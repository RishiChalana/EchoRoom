---
name: EchoRoom
tagline: Practice how you speak. Before it matters.
register: product
description: >
  EchoRoom is a real-time AI communication coaching platform. Users select
  a practice context (Technical, Interview, Presentation, General), speak
  into the microphone, and receive live transcription with engagement scoring.
  When the session ends, an AI coach generates a full report: an overall score,
  an engagement timeline, specific insights with evidence, and before/after
  rewrite suggestions.

target_users:
  - Engineers rehearsing architecture talks or tech demos
  - Job seekers practicing for interviews
  - Founders rehearsing investor pitches
  - Educators improving lecture delivery
  - Anyone who wants to communicate more clearly under pressure

core_flows:
  - Land → Sign in with Google/GitHub → Dashboard
  - Dashboard → Choose a room → Name the session → Start recording
  - Speak → see live transcription + engagement gauge → End session
  - Report page: score, timeline, AI coaching insights, precision edits

emotional_arc: >
  Arrives with a specific goal and mild anxiety → Feels the room's
  distinct atmosphere while practicing → Ends with relief → Report
  delivers a genuine "yes, that's what I need to fix" moment
---
